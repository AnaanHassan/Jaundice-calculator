Risk Factor Definitions

AAP Guideline (≥35 wks)

  • Isoimmune hemolytic disease, G6PD deficiency
  • Sepsis
  • Significant clinical instability in the previous 24 hours
  • Serum albumin <3.0 g/dL

Preterm Weight-Based Guideline

A "sick" neonate is defined by:

  • Rapidly rising TSB levels (>0.5 mg/dL/hr)
  • Clinical instability, sepsis, or asphyxia

NICE Guideline

  • Does not use a quantitative risk adjustment. Risk should be assessed qualitatively by the clinician.
